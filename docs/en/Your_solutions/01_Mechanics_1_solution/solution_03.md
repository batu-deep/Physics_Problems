
## Question 3 – Conservation of Energy

**Problem:**  
A pendulum (L = 1 m) is released from 15°. Find the speed at the bottom.

---

**Step 1:**  
Use energy conservation:  
Potential → kinetic

**Step 2:**  
Height difference:  
h = L(1 − cosθ)

**Step 3:**  
h ≈ 0.034 m

**Step 4:**  
v = √(2gh)

**Step 5:**  
v ≈ 0.82 m/s  
→ Small angle → small speed.

**Final Answer:**  
v ≈ 0.82 m/s
