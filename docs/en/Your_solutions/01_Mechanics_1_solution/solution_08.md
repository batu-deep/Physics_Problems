## Question 8 – Circular Motion

**Problem:**  
Find centripetal acceleration at Earth’s equator.

---

**Step 1: Use formula**  
a = ω²R

---

**Step 2: Find angular velocity**  
Earth rotates once every 24 hours:

ω = 2π / (86400 s)

---

**Step 3: Substitute radius**  
R = 6.378 × 10⁶ m

---

**Step 4: Calculate acceleration**  
a ≈ 0.034 m/s²

---

**Interpretation:**  
This is very small compared to gravity → we don’t feel it.

---

**Final Answer:**  
a ≈ 0.034 m/s²
