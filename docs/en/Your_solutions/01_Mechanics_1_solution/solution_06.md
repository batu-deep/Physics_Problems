## Question 6 – Variable Velocity

**Problem:**  
v(t) = t² + 2t − 5, with x(0) = 4. Find position and acceleration at t = 3.

---

**Step 1: Find acceleration**  
Acceleration is derivative of velocity:

a(t) = dv/dt = 2t + 2

---

**Step 2: Evaluate acceleration at t = 3**  
a(3) = 2(3) + 2 = 8 m/s²

---

**Step 3: Find position function**  
Position is integral of velocity:

x(t) = ∫(t² + 2t − 5) dt  
= (1/3)t³ + t² − 5t + C

---

**Step 4: Use initial condition**  
x(0) = 4 → C = 4

---

**Step 5: Evaluate position at t = 3**  
x(3) = (1/3)(27) + 9 − 15 + 4  
= 9 + 9 − 15 + 4 = 7

---

**Interpretation:**  
Even though velocity changes nonlinearly, we can always integrate to find position.

---

**Final Answer:**  
x(3) = 7  
a(3) = 8 m/s²
