## Question 1 – Gravitational Dependence

### (a) Period on the Moon

**Step 1:**  
Use pendulum formula:  
T = 2π√(L/g) → T ∝ 1/√g

**Step 2:**  
Compare Earth and Moon:
T_M = T_E √(g_E / g_M)

**Step 3:**  
g_M = g_E / 6 → T_M = 4√6

**Step 4:**  
T_M ≈ 4 × 2.45 = 9.8 s

**Final Answer:**  
T_M ≈ 9.8 s

### (b) Length for T = 1 s

**Step 1:**  
T = 2π√(L/g)

**Step 2:**  
Rearrange:
L = gT² / (4π²)

**Step 3:**  
L = 9.81 / (4π²)

**Step 4:**  
L ≈ 0.25 m

**Final Answer:**  
L ≈ 0.25 m
