## Question 1 – Projectile Motion

**Problem:**  
A projectile is launched at 100 m/s and 37°.

---

### (a) Differential Equations

**Step 1:**  
Separate motion into x and y directions.  
→ Because forces act differently in each direction.

**Step 2:**  
Horizontal direction: no acceleration (no air resistance)  
d²x/dt² = 0

**Step 3:**  
Vertical direction: only gravity acts downward  
d²y/dt² = −g

---

### (b) Time of Flight

**Step 1:**  
Find vertical velocity component:  
v_y0 = v₀ sinθ = 100 × sin37° ≈ 60 m/s

**Step 2:**  
Use symmetry: total time = 2 × time to reach top

**Step 3:**  
t_up = v_y0 / g ≈ 60 / 9.81 ≈ 6.12 s

**Step 4:**  
Total time: T = 2 × 6.12 ≈ 12.24 s

---

### (c) Maximum Height

**Step 1:**  
Use formula:  
h_max = v_y0² / (2g)

**Step 2:**  
h_max ≈ 60² / (2×9.81) ≈ 183.5 m

---

### (d) Range

**Step 1:**  
Horizontal velocity:  
v_x = 100 cos37° ≈ 80 m/s

**Step 2:**  
Range = v_x × T

**Step 3:**  
R ≈ 80 × 12.24 ≈ 979 m

---

**Final Answers:**  
T ≈ 12.2 s  
h ≈ 183 m  
R ≈ 979 m
