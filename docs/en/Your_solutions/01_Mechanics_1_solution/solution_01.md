## Question 1 – Gravitational Dependence

**Problem:**  
A simple pendulum has a period of 4 seconds on Earth. What would its period be on the Moon, where gravity is 1/6 of Earth's?  
What length is required for a pendulum to have a period of 1 second on Earth?

---

### (a) Period on the Moon

**Step 1:**  
Start with the pendulum formula:  
T = 2π√(L/g)  
→ Period depends on gravity as 1/√g.

**Step 2:**  
Use ratio since length is unchanged:  
T_M / T_E = √(g_E / g_M)

**Step 3:**  
g_M = g_E / 6 → substitute:

T_M = 4√6

**Step 4:**  
T_M ≈ 9.8 s  
→ Lower gravity → longer period.

**Final Answer:**  
T_M ≈ 9.8 s

---

### (b) Length for T = 1 s

**Step 1:**  
T = 2π√(L/g)

**Step 2:**  
Solve for L:  
L = gT² / (4π²)

**Step 3:**  
Substitute values:

L ≈ 0.25 m

**Final Answer:**  
L ≈ 0.25 m
