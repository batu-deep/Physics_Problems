## Question 9 – Momentum Comparison

**Problem:**  
Compare momentum of:
- 2 g fly at 10 m/s  
- 60 g tennis ball at 1 m/s

---

**Step 1: Convert units**  
Fly = 0.002 kg  
Ball = 0.06 kg

---

**Step 2: Use momentum formula**  
p = mv

---

**Step 3: Calculate fly momentum**  
p = 0.002 × 10 = 0.02 kg·m/s

---

**Step 4: Calculate ball momentum**  
p = 0.06 × 1 = 0.06 kg·m/s

---

**Step 5: Compare values**  
0.06 > 0.02

---

**Interpretation:**  
Even though fly is faster, mass dominates → ball has more momentum.

---

**Final Answer:**  
Tennis ball has greater momentum
