## Question 2 – Harmonic Motion

**Problem:**  
A 10 kg mass oscillates as x(t) = 0.2 cos(10πt). Find the spring constant and total energy.

---

**Step 1:**  
Compare with SHM form:  
x = A cos(ωt) → ω = 10π

**Step 2:**  
Use relation:  
ω = √(k/m) → k = mω²

**Step 3:**  
k ≈ 9869 N/m  
→ Higher ω means stiffer spring.

**Step 4:**  
Energy formula:  
E = (1/2)kA²

**Step 5:**  
E ≈ 197 J  
→ Energy depends on amplitude squared.

**Final Answer:**  
k ≈ 9.87×10³ N/m  
E ≈ 197 J
