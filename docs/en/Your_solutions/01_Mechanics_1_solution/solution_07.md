## Question 7 – Elimination of Time & Acceleration

**Problem:**  
x(t) = 2t², y(t) = 3t³

---

**Step 1: Eliminate parameter t**  
From x = 2t² → t = √(x/2)

---

**Step 2: Substitute into y**  
y = 3t³ = 3(x/2)^(3/2)

---

**Step 3: Interpret trajectory**  
This is a nonlinear curve → not a straight line.

---

**Step 4: Find velocity vector**  
v = (dx/dt, dy/dt)

dx/dt = 4t  
dy/dt = 9t²  

v = (4t, 9t²)

---

**Step 5: Speed (magnitude)**  
|v| = √[(4t)² + (9t²)²]  
= √(16t² + 81t⁴)

---

**Step 6: Acceleration vector**  
a = derivative of velocity:

a = (4, 18t)

---

**Step 7: Acceleration magnitude**  
|a| = √(4² + (18t)²) = √(16 + 324t²)

---

**Step 8: Check if acceleration is constant**  
It depends on t → NOT constant

---

**Interpretation:**  
Motion becomes more curved and acceleration increases over time.

---

**Final Answer:**  
y = 3(x/2)^(3/2)  
Acceleration is not constant
