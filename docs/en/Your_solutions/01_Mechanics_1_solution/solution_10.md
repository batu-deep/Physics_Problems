## Question 10 – Kinematics (Parametric Motion)

**Problem:**  
r(t) = (a cos(ωt), b sin(ωt), bt)

---

### (a) Trajectory

**Step 1:**  
From x and y:

x = a cos(ωt)  
y = b sin(ωt)

---

**Step 2:**  
Eliminate t using identity:

(x/a)² + (y/b)² = 1

---

**Step 3:**  
z = bt → increases linearly

---

**Interpretation:**  
Motion is an ellipse in xy-plane + linear motion in z → helical trajectory.

---

### (b) Path Length

**Step 1:**  
Speed = |v| where v = dr/dt

---

**Step 2:**  
Differentiate components:

v = (−aω sin(ωt), bω cos(ωt), b)

---

**Step 3:**  
Speed magnitude:

|v| = √[(aω sin)² + (bω cos)² + b²]

---

**Step 4:**  
Length = ∫|v| dt from 0 to t₀

---

### (c) Interpretation

- If a = b → circular helix  
- If b = 0 → flat ellipse  
- If ω = 0 → straight line

---

**Final Answer:**  
Trajectory is an elliptical helix
