# Solution 10 — Infinite Series

Horizontal movement:

1 − 1/3 + 1/5 − 1/7 + ...

This series converges to:

π / 4

Vertical movement:

1/2 − 1/4 + 1/6 − 1/8 + ...

This equals:

(1/2) ln(2)

Final position:

x = π / 4 ≈ 0.785  
y = (1/2) ln(2) ≈ 0.347

Final answer:

Final position ≈ (0.785 , 0.347)
