# Solution 03 — Proportionality

Universal Law of Gravitation:

F = G (m₁m₂ / r²)

Changes:

r → 2r  
m₁ → m₁/2  
m₂ → m₂/2

Substitute:

F' = G ((m₁/2)(m₂/2) / (2r)²)

F' = G (m₁m₂ / (4 × 4r²))

F' = G (m₁m₂ / 16r²)

Original:

F = G (m₁m₂ / r²)

Therefore:

F' = F / 16

Final answer:

The gravitational force becomes **16 times smaller**.
