# Solution 05 — Trigonometry

Magnitude of vector A = 15  
Angle θ = 60°

Components:

Ax = A cosθ  
Ay = A sinθ

Horizontal component:

Ax = 15 cos60°  
Ax = 15 × 0.5  
Ax = 7.5

Vertical component:

Ay = 15 sin60°  
Ay = 15 × 0.866  
Ay ≈ 12.99

Final answer:

Ax = 7.5  
Ay ≈ 12.99
