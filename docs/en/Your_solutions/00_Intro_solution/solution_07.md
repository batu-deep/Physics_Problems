# Solution 07 — Logic & Series

Distance to wall = 10 m  
Bicycle speed = 1 m/s

Time until bicycle hits wall:

t = distance / speed

t = 10 / 1  
t = 10 s

Fly speed = 2 m/s

Distance traveled by fly:

d = vt

d = 2 × 10  
d = 20 m

Final answer:

The fly travels **20 meters**.
