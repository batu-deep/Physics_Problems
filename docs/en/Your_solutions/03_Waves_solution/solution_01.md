# 🌊 Question 1 – Wave Properties

## Problem
A sound wave has frequency 440 Hz.  
Find wavelength in air (343 m/s) and water (1482 m/s).

---

## Step 1: Recall the wave relation

We start from the basic equation:

v = fλ

This relates speed, frequency, and wavelength.

---

## Step 2: Solve for wavelength

We rearrange:

λ = v / f

We do this because we are given speed and frequency, and we want wavelength.

---

## Step 3: Calculate wavelength in air

λ_air = 343 / 440 ≈ 0.78 m

This gives the spacing between wave peaks in air.

---

## Step 4: Calculate wavelength in water

λ_water = 1482 / 440 ≈ 3.37 m

The larger speed in water produces a larger wavelength.

---

## Step 5: Interpret the result

Frequency stays constant, but wavelength changes with speed.

Higher speed → larger wavelength.

---

## Final Answer

λ_air ≈ 0.78 m  
λ_water ≈ 3.37 m
