# 🌊 Question 6 – Wave Equation Analysis

## Problem
y(x,t) = 0.05 sin(2πx − 50πt)

---

## Step 1: Identify amplitude

The coefficient in front of sine is the amplitude:

A = 0.05 m

---

## Step 2: Identify wave number

k = 2π → λ = 2π/k = 1 m

---

## Step 3: Identify angular frequency

ω = 50π → f = ω/(2π) = 25 Hz

---

## Step 4: Calculate wave speed

v = fλ = 25 × 1 = 25 m/s

---

## Step 5: Interpret the equation

All wave properties are encoded directly in the sine function’s arguments.

---

## Final Answer

A = 0.05 m  
λ = 1 m  
f = 25 Hz  
v = 25 m/s
