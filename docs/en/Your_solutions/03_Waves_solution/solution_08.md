# 🌊 Question 8 – Wave Equation Check

## Problem
Determine which functions represent valid traveling waves.

---

## Step 1: Recall wave condition

A traveling wave must depend on:

x ± vt

---

## Step 2: Analyze function (a)

cos(kx² − ωt) → nonlinear in x → does not satisfy wave equation

---

## Step 3: Analyze function (b)

(x − vt)² → depends on correct form → valid

---

## Step 4: Analyze function (c)

log(x + vt) → also depends on correct form → valid

---

## Step 5: Interpret

Only functions depending on (x ± vt) represent traveling waves.

---

## Final Answer

(b) and (c) are valid
