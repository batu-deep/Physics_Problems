# 🌊 Question 7 – Standing Wave Modes

## Problem
A standing wave with four antinodes exists on a string of length 80 cm.

---

## Step 1: Interpret number of antinodes

The number of antinodes corresponds to the harmonic number:

n = 4

---

## Step 2: Use standing wave relation

L = nλ/2

This relation comes from fitting wave segments into the string.

---

## Step 3: Solve for wavelength

λ = 2L/n

---

## Step 4: Substitute values

λ = 2 × 0.8 / 4 = 0.4 m

---

## Step 5: Interpret the result

Higher modes correspond to shorter wavelengths.

---

## Final Answer

λ = 0.4 m
