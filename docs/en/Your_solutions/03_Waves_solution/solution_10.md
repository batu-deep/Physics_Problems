# 🌊 Question 10 – Wave Sources Animation (Conceptual)

## Problem
Wave from point sources with decay.

---

## Step 1: Understand equation

u(r,t) = A / |r − r₀|^α × sin(...)

---

## Step 2: Interpret terms

- Distance term → amplitude decay  
- Sine → oscillation  

---

## Step 3: Superposition

Total wave = sum of all sources

---

## Step 4: Effect of α

- α = 0 → no decay  
- α = 2 → strong decay  

---

## Step 5: Physical meaning

Waves weaken as they spread from source.

---

## Final Insight

Wave behavior depends on distance and interference.
