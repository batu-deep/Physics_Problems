# 🌊 Question 5 – Echo Ranging

## Problem
An echo is heard 1 second after shouting. Find distance to the cliff.

---

## Step 1: Understand the motion of sound

The sound travels from the person to the cliff and then reflects back. Therefore, the measured time corresponds to a round trip.

---

## Step 2: Calculate total distance traveled

Using:

distance = speed × time

Total distance = 343 × 1 = 343 m

---

## Step 3: Find one-way distance

Since the sound travels twice the distance:

d = 343 / 2 = 171.5 m

---

## Step 4: Interpret the result

The division by 2 is essential because the time includes both outgoing and returning paths.

---

## Final Answer

d ≈ 171.5 m
