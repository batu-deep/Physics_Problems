# 🌊 Question 9 – Damped Oscillator (Conceptual)

## Problem
Analyze damped harmonic motion.

---

## Step 1: Recognize equation

m d²x/dt² + b dx/dt + kx = 0

This describes a system losing energy over time.

---

## Step 2: Identify damping types

- Underdamped → oscillatory decay  
- Critically damped → fastest return without oscillation  
- Overdamped → slow return  

---

## Step 3: General solution

Depends on damping ratio:

x(t) = e^(−γt)(A cosωt + B sinωt)

---

## Step 4: Interpret parameter b

Increasing b increases energy loss and reduces oscillation.

---

## Step 5: Physical meaning

System gradually loses energy due to resistance.

---

## Final Insight

Damping controls how quickly motion dies out.
