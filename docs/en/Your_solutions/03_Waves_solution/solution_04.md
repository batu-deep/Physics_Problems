# 🌊 Question 4 – Phase Difference

## Problem
Find the phase difference between two points separated by λ/3.

---

## Step 1: Recall the phase-distance relationship

Phase difference is related to spatial separation by:

Δφ = (2π/λ) × Δx

This expresses how phase changes along the wave.

---

## Step 2: Substitute the given distance

Δx = λ/3

So:

Δφ = (2π/λ)(λ/3)

---

## Step 3: Simplify the expression

The λ cancels:

Δφ = 2π/3

---

## Step 4: Interpret the result

This means the two points are not in phase; they differ by one-third of a full cycle.

---

## Final Answer

Δφ = 2π/3 radians
