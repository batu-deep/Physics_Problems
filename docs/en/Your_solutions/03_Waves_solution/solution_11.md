# 🌊 Question 11 – Two-Slit Interference

## Problem
Simulate Young’s experiment.

---

## Step 1: Understand setup

Two coherent sources produce overlapping waves.

---

## Step 2: Superposition principle

Total wave = sum of both waves

---

## Step 3: Constructive interference

Occurs when path difference = nλ

---

## Step 4: Destructive interference

Occurs when path difference = (n + 1/2)λ

---

## Step 5: Effect of parameters

- Larger d → more fringes  
- Larger λ → wider spacing  

---

## Final Insight

Interference pattern comes from phase differences.
